LISTEN. GUESS. REPEAT.

A little sound goes a long way.

  1. In Spotify, open a public playlist and choose Share → Copy link to playlist. Paste it above.
  2. Press play. Your first clue is just 0.1 seconds.
  3. Type a title or artist and choose a song. A wrong guess or skip unlocks a longer clip.
  4. Get it early for more points. Each correct round adds to your streak.

Difficulty changes the clip lengths and number of attempts. Changing difficulty starts a fresh round. Songs shuffle without repeating until the playlist is exhausted.

Only publicly available previews are imported. Private playlists, unavailable previews, and some Spotify-curated playlists cannot be used.
